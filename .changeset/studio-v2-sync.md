Studio keeps up with the folder on disk: autosave (1 s after typing, and at once when the window loses focus), saves that can't overwrite a change Studio hasn't seen (a conflict banner instead), agent edits applied in place, a resync after reconnecting, Follow that never switches files while you type or during a burst of changes, and Back/Forward through the files you looked at.
